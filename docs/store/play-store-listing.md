## Full description (max 4000)

AutoVerse turns your Android head unit (or phone) into a clean, fast car
cockpit. One glance shows the road map, what's playing and how fast you're
going — and every app is one tap away.

★ A real cockpit on your home screen
• Live map that follows your car — smooth, GPU-rendered, heading-up while you drive
• Music panel for Spotify, YouTube Music and other players: play/pause, skip, artwork and the player's own queue
• GPS speedometer, with an honest "waiting for GPS / signal lost" state instead of fake numbers
• Incoming calls appear on Home while they ring (optional)

★ Navigation that just works
• One tap opens Google Maps — or Waze, or the map app you prefer
• Type a destination and go; recent and saved places one tap away
• Maps you have already viewed stay available offline

★ All your apps
• Every installed app with search and categories
• "Recently Used" keeps your most-used apps in reach

★ Built for the car
• Designed for slower head units: minimal animation, capped frame rate, light map style
• 12 themes included (Cobalt, Obsidian, Neon Pulse, Sakura, Neon Tokyo, Gotham, Hot Rod and more)
• Speed page with trip distance, trip time, top speed and weather
• OBD-II support: connect a Bluetooth ELM327 adapter for RPM, coolant temperature, fuel level and fault codes

★ Private by design
• No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics
• Your location, apps and destinations stay on your device
